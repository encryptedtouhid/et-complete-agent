# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## TL;DR

- Be respectful. Disagree about code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Public criticism stays focused on the work; private criticism stays private.

## Reporting

Conduct issues that you don't want to raise publicly: email
**me@tuhidulhossain.com**. Reports are confidential. The maintainers will
respond within 5 business days.

Reports involving the maintainers themselves: email the same address with a
clear subject line; an independent reviewer will be brought in.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may warn, restrict, or ban contributors whose behaviour
violates this code. The Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe the severity ladder.
